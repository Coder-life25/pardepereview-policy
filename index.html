PardePeReview Publisher - Privacy and data deletion

Effective: 1 November 2026

PardePeReview Publisher is the account owner's internal tool for preparing Hindi cinema content and publishing to the PardePereview Facebook Page and @pardepereview Instagram professional account. It does not offer sign-in or publishing services to other customers.

Information used The tool uses the account identity, Page and Instagram profile identifiers, connected-asset information, post metadata and the publishing permissions expressly granted by the owner. It stores access credentials, prepared media, publication identifiers and operational diagnostics needed to publish and avoid duplicate posts. It does not request access to private messages, contacts, payment information or personal Facebook timelines.

Purpose and sharing Information is used to verify the intended accounts, prepare and publish authorized content, track publication receipts and diagnose failures. Meta receives the posts and media selected for publication. GitHub Actions runs the publishing jobs and holds encrypted Actions credentials; an access-restricted local setup directory also retains setup credentials. Official public film information may be sent to Google's Gemini service for script generation, and narration text to Microsoft's speech service. Meta access credentials are not sent to these content-generation services. The tool does not sell account data or use it for advertising audiences.

Storage and retention GitHub media/diagnostic artifacts are configured to expire after two days. Publication receipts, selected scripts and setup credentials remain until the owner removes them or ends the integration. Public posts remain on the platform until removed using its controls. Revocation may prevent further access but does not itself erase previously published posts or local copies. Access tokens can expire or be revoked; uninterrupted access is not guaranteed.

Control and deletion The owner can revoke PardePeReview Publisher under Facebook Settings > Business integrations. To remove app-held data, the owner should also delete its Facebook/Instagram Actions secrets, ignored local OAuth setup files and retained operational records, then remove any published posts they no longer want. A deletion request can be submitted through the contact option on the official PardePereview Facebook Page: https://www.facebook.com/61596040140283 . Requests are reviewed by the account owner. This notice does not require sharing passwords or verification codes.

Changes The notice will be updated if the tool's data use changes. Meta, GitHub, Google and Microsoft operate under their own privacy terms.
